Baseli map

Teaching list only (not official). Source: DSEDJ Requirements of Basic Academic Attainments and junior/senior natural-science curriculum guidance. Physics-related items show code + full text; links only where a lab/chapter exists.

Senior natural-science Baseli is not a full mechanics catalogue; senior hooks below are marked “suggested pairing / stream” for teaching alignment only.

Junior (physical science · physics-related)

  • B-3-6

    Be able to describe the meaning of speed and average speed.

    No interactive chapter yet
  • B-3-7

    Be able to identify common forces in daily life such as gravity, friction, elasticity, and state their effects.

    No interactive chapter yet
  • B-3-8

    Know the relationship between mass and gravity, and be able to do basic calculation.

    No interactive chapter yet
  • B-3-9

    Be able to specify the conditions for the balance between two forces.

    No interactive chapter yet
  • B-3-10

    Understand that force is the cause of change in the state of object motion; and be able to explain some common phenomena with the concept of inertia.

    No interactive chapter yet
  • B-3-11

    Understand the concept of pressure, and the methods to change pressure.

    Open → Fluids
  • B-3-12

    Be able to describe the characteristics of liquid pressure; and explain Archimedes’ principle and the conditions of floating and sinking.

    Open → Fluids
  • B-3-13

    Know the measurement of air pressure, and understand the change of air pressure as well as its effects on daily life.

    Open → Fluids
  • B-3-14

    Understand the three elements of sound, condition for the production and spreading of sound; be able to state the hazards of noise and methods of noise abatement.

    No interactive chapter yet
  • B-3-15

    Be able to briefly state the characteristics of light propagation in the same homogeneous medium.

    Open → Optics 3D lab
  • B-3-16

    Recognise the laws of reflection and refraction of light, and be able to illustrate their practical application.

    Open → Optics 3D lab
  • B-3-17

    Be able to draw simple light path diagrams of light reflection and refraction.

    Open → Optics 3D lab
  • B-3-18

    Understand dispersion of light and the mixture of coloured lights.

    Open → Optics 3D lab
  • B-3-19

    Recognise electric charge and its basic properties; understand the basic structure of a circuit; be able to make a series circuit and a parallel circuit, and draw circuit diagrams.

    Open → Circuits
  • B-3-20

    Know how to use the ammeter and voltmeter.

    Open → Circuits
  • B-3-21

    Understand Ohm’s law, and be able to do simple calculations.

    Open → Circuits
  • B-3-22

    Be able to briefly describe the heating effect of electric current and the application of fuses.

    Open → Circuits
  • B-3-23

    Understand semiconductors and superconductors as well as the impact of their application on society.

    Open → Circuits
  • B-3-24

    Be able to illustrate the relationship between electric power, current and voltage.

    Open → Circuits
  • B-3-25

    Be able to distinguish between the rated power and actual power of an electrical appliance.

    Open → Circuits
  • B-3-26

    Be able to indicate the polarity of the magnet, and be able to draw common magnetic induction lines to indicate the magnetic field.

    Open → Magnetism
  • B-3-27

    Have a preliminary understanding of the magnetic field around the energised conductor, and the characteristics of the magnetic field around the energised solenoid.

    Open → Magnetism
  • B-3-28

    Be able to describe the application of electromagnetic wave, and its influence on human life and social development.

    Open → Magnetism
  • B-4-1

    Understand energy and its diverse forms of existence; know energy transfer and energy conversion, and be able to enumerate simple energy conversion devices such as battery, motor and generator.

    Open → Work & energy
  • B-4-2

    Be able to describe the significance of work and power, and know the process of work is the process of energy conversion or energy transfer.

    Open → Work & energy
  • B-4-3

    Understand the concepts of kinetic energy and potential energy, and be able to name the factors that affect the amount of them.

    Open → Work & energy
  • B-4-4

    Briefly describe the history of the discovery of the Law of Conservation of Energy, and be able to analyse physical phenomena using the view of energy conversion as well as the law of conservation of energy.

    Open → Work & energy
  • B-4-5

    Be able to state the classification and characteristics of different energy resources, and briefly describe the relationship between energy resources and human existence as well as sustainable social development.

    Open → Work & energy
